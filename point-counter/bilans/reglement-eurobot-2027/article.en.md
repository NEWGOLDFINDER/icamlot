The **official version 1.0 of the Eurobot 2027 rules** has just been released by Planète Sciences. The theme of this 34th edition: **the legend of King Arthur and Camelot**. Robots think they are knights, every team owns "the real" Grail, and it has to be kept safe behind the walls of their castle!

### The table

The playing area is still a 3 m × 2 m board. Each team has a **castle**: a courtyard with the throne room (the robot's start and end zone), surrounded by ramparts and moats. On the table, **30 stones** (cardboard boxes of 320 × 110 × 110 mm, marked with an ArUco tag) wait in quarries, stacked in threes.

### The four actions

- **Building Camelot**: pick up the stones and build the castle following the plans. A **wall** is 3 stones lying flat and stacked, a **tower** is 1 standing stone, and a **gate** is 2 standing stones topped with a lintel. Each stone placed scores 1 point, a wall or a tower +5, and the single gate +20.
- **There can be only one, and it's mine!**: each team builds its own Grail. During the match, the robot must raise it as high as possible inside the castle: on a stone, a building, or even on its own roof. 5 points if it is in the castle, then 5 points per level, up to 4.
- **Return of the King**: at the end of the match, the main robot goes back to its throne room (5 points if partly inside, 10 if fully inside).
- **Charge!**: from the 85th second, the SIMAs (our "knights") set off to attack the opponent's castle. They score 5 points per opponent moat occupied and 5 points if they attack an opponent SIMA. Robots can also **bombard** the opponent's courtyard with foam cannonballs: 5 points for one ball, +15 from three.

As every year, all actions are independent and none is mandatory: the rules recommend aiming for simple, reliable systems on a limited number of actions.

### What changed since the beta

The scoring is now set. Quarries hold stones **lying flat and stacked** (no longer standing), the orientation of the ArUco tags is specified, and wall zones grow to 200 × 400 mm. As for the Grail, it must be larger than a 60 mm cube, and touching the opponent's Grail is now forbidden.

### Calculate your points

To plan your strategy or check a match, we have put a **[2027 points calculator](calculateur.html?lang=en)** online: a few taps give you your score.

The full rules are available on [eurobot.org](https://www.eurobot.org/) and questions go to the [official FAQ](https://www.eurobot.org/faq/). See you on the table!
