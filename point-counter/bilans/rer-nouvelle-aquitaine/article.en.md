The **RER Nouvelle-Aquitaine**, organised by Eirbot, brought together the region's teams on 21 March 2026. It was the first outing of the Opossum 2026 robot, along with our first SIMA prototypes.

The day let us run test after test on a practice table and highlight several points to improve before the French Robotics Cup.

We also had the chance to visit the amazing ENSEIRB-MATMECA fablab and Eirbot's workshop, and above all to chat with the other teams — whom we'll be glad to see again at the French Robotics Cup.

A big thank you to Eirbot for organising this great day!
