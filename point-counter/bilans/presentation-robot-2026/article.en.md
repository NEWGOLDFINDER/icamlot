Here is **Team Opossum's main robot** for the 2026 French Robotics Cup.

It's a **4-wheel holonomic** robot, driven by **M2006 P36** motors that let it reach **1.5 m/s** — enough to move fast and in every direction.

# Finding its way and seeing the game
For navigation, it carries an **A2M12 lidar** used both for **opponent avoidance** and for **localization by triangulation on the fixed beacons**.

To spot the game elements of the 2026 edition, it also has **three JeVois Pro cameras**.

# The electronics
The robot relies on two complementary brains: a **Raspberry Pi 4** for high-level strategy, and a **Zynq-7000** dedicated to motor control and actuator management.

# Grasping
For manipulation, it is fitted with **8 suction grippers**, able to grab and flip the rules' "jenga" pieces.

# Dynamic match scripts
Another big novelty for us this year: the introduction of **fully dynamic match scripts**. Rather than following a fixed sequence, the robot **adapts intelligently to what happens during the match** and chooses its actions based on the situation.

A complete and versatile robot, designed to play autonomously and opportunistically!
